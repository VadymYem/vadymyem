## About

I am **Vadym Yemelianov**, also known as **AuthorChe** — a Ukrainian developer and creative technologist with a background in music, vocal performance, education, composition, poetry, and software development.

My work combines two areas that are often treated separately:

- **Technology** — web applications, browser APIs, automation, bots, local-first architecture, open-source software, and privacy-focused products.
- **Creative practice** — music, vocal performance, composition, poetry, writing, and digital culture.

I am especially interested in building tools that are useful, technically serious, visually distinctive, and accessible without unnecessary platform dependence.

> **Technology should expand creative freedom, not replace it.**

### OpenVox

**OpenVox** is a free and open-source browser-based studio for singers, vocal teachers, musicians, students, and choirs.

It is designed as a **privacy-first, local-first music technology platform** where core audio analysis can run directly in the browser without requiring a dedicated server-side processing backend.

OpenVox brings together:

- real-time pitch detection and vocal monitoring;
- structured vocal training and Vocal Academy workflows;
- sight-singing, ear-training, rhythm, breathing, and pitch exercises;
- instrument tuning with multiple presets and custom tunings;
- backing-track rehearsal tools;
- multitrack audio workflows;
- score editing and notation tools;
- MusicXML and MIDI import/export workflows;
- choir rehearsal and sectional practice;
- voice-to-score transcription;
- vocal range, stability, and vibrato analysis;
- a professional browser-based audio lab;
- local recording, analysis, project storage, and export;
- responsive desktop and mobile interfaces;
- Progressive Web App support.

The project uses modern browser technology including:

`React` · `TypeScript` · `Vite` · `Web Audio API` · `AudioWorklet` · `WebAssembly` · `IndexedDB` · `OfflineAudioContext` · `MediaRecorder` · `PWA`

OpenVox is built around a simple principle:

> **Your voice should not need to leave your device to be analyzed.**

## Development Principles

I try to build software around a few consistent principles.

### Local first where practical

Sensitive or personal data should not be sent to a server when the same task can reasonably be completed on the user's device.

### Open by default

Open-source projects should be understandable, auditable, documented, and maintainable.

### Useful before decorative

Visual design matters, but functionality, reliability, accessibility, and clear behavior matter more.

### Technology with identity

I prefer projects with a clear point of view instead of anonymous copies of existing products.

### Cross-disciplinary work

The most interesting products often appear where different areas meet:

**software + music + education + culture + creativity**
